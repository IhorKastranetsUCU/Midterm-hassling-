Probability and Statistics: Midterm 2 preparation @ UCU

Spring 2018

I am ready for Midterm 2 if
• I remember the definitions of:
 discrete random variable
 probability mass function (p.m.f.)
 cumulative distribution function (c.m.f.)
 continuous random variable
 probability density function (p.m.f.)
 expectation, moments, variance, covariance
 probability generating function (p.g.f.) and moment generating function (m.g.f.) and their properties
• I know the following distributions (p.m.f./p.d.f.) and their basic properties:
 binomial B(n, p)
 Poisson B(λ)
 geometric G (p)
 uniform U (a, b)
 normal N (µ, σ 2 )
 exponential E (λ)
• I understand the following notions:
 joint distribution of r.v.’s (joint p.m.f./p.d.f., joint c.d.f.)
 marginal distribution
 independence of r.v.’s
 covariance and correlation
 conditional distribution of X given that Y = y (conditional p.m.f./p.d.f.)
 distribution of transformed r.v.’s (or random vectors)
• I can:
 recognise standard discrete and continuous distributions
 construct random variables corresponding to given experiments
 calculate distributions of random variables
 calculate expectation, variance, and moments of a r.v.
 calculate joint and marginal distributions of r.v.’s and their covariance
 determine whether r.v.’s are independent
 determine the conditional distribution of a r.v. given the value of the other r.v.
 determine distribution of transformations of r.v.’s (e.g. of the sum X + Y )
 solve standard problems about r.v.’s and their distributions (e.g. from the problem sets 4–6)

Yes, I am now ready for Midterm 2!
