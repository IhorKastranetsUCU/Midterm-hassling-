Problem 5. Alice and Bob enter simultaneously the grocery shop. Assume that their shopping times are
independent r.v.’s X and Y respectively with exponential distribution of mean value 15 minutes.
(a) What is the distribution of the r.v. M := min(X, Y ) for the smaller of the two shopping times? What
is the expected value for the smaller shopping time?
(b) What is the distribution of the time W := |X − Y | that the first to finish shopping will wait for his/her
friend?
(c) Check that E(M ) + E(W ) is larger than 15 min; is not there any contradiction there?
(d) Justify the strong memorylessness property: given that Alice is the second to finish her shopping, the
difference X − Y has the same distribution as X.

You walk into the station every morning between 7:10 and 7:30 a.m., with the time in this interval being a
random variable with given PDF. Let X be the elapsed time, in minutes, between 7:10 and the time of your
arrival. Let Y be the time that you have to wait until you board a train. Calculate the c.d.f. of Y in terms
of the c.d.f. of X and differentiate to obtain a formula for the p.d.f. of Y .
