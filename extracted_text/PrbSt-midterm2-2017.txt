Problem 3 (10pt). Assume that Z has the standard normal distribution N (0, 1)
and Y = 3 − 2Z
(a) Determine the cumulative distribution function of Y in terms of Φ.
(b) Find the probability density function of Y and identify the distribution of Y .
(c) Express in terms of Φ the probability that Y ≤ 1.
(d) Express in terms of Φ the probability that Y > 5.
Problem 4 (10pt). Random variables X1 and X2 are independent and have Poisson
distribution P(λ).
(a) Find E(X1 − X2 ) and Var(X1 − X2 ).
(b) Find the probability generating function of X1 .
(c) Using (b), prove that Y := X1 + X2 has distribution P(2λ).
(d) Prove that the conditional distribution (X1 | Y = n) of X1 given that Y = n is
binomial B( 21 ).
Problem 5 (10pt). There are two types of batteries in a bin. When in use, type i
batteries last (in hours) an exponentially distributed time with rate λi , i = 1, 2. A
battery that is randomly chosen from the bin will be a type i battery with probability
pi , p1 + p2 = 1.
(a) What is the probability that a randomly chosen battery is still operating after
t hours of use?
(b) Calculate the expected value of operating time for a randomly chosen battery.
(c) If a randomly chosen battery is still operating after t hours of use, what is the
probability that it will still be operating after an additional s hours?
